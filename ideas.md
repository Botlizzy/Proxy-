## Selected approach: Editorial Signal

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial design crossed with Swiss modernism and the tactile clarity of an engineer's notebook.

### Core Principles
1. Make the work the hero through strong typographic hierarchy and confident asymmetry.
2. Balance human warmth with technical precision: personal copy sits beside sharp metadata and project signals.
3. Use restrained surfaces, thin rules, and deliberate color blocks instead of generic cards and loud gradients.
4. Let motion feel physical and authored: reveal, glide, and settle — never bounce without purpose.

### Color Philosophy
The base is an ink-black blue that gives the page seriousness and makes the signature saffron accent feel earned. Saffron is used like a highlighter in a notebook: for calls to action, active states, and moments that deserve attention. Cream text and muted steel-blue secondary tones keep the experience warm and readable without becoming soft.

### Layout Paradigm
A left-anchored editorial rail establishes identity while content moves through offset sections. The hero uses a split composition with a large statement, a vertical index, and a workbench-style profile panel. Project entries alternate emphasis rather than living in identical cards.

### Signature Elements
- A vertical index rail with section numbers and saffron active markers.
- Fine measurement lines and small uppercase annotations that make the page feel like an annotated studio wall.
- A live-looking availability pulse and cursor-like accents that humanize the technical presentation.

### Interaction Philosophy
Every interaction should clarify intent. Hover states expose context, buttons feel tactile, and links acknowledge that the visitor is moving through a real body of work. Motion reinforces hierarchy instead of competing with it.

### Animation
Entrances use short opacity and translate transitions with staggered delays of 40–70ms. Project images lift by a few pixels on hover while their metadata shifts in saffron. The hero background includes a slow, almost imperceptible grain drift and a measured cursor-follow glow. All non-essential motion is disabled for prefers-reduced-motion users.

### Typography System
Use Space Grotesk for display text and IBM Plex Mono for labels, metadata, navigation, and technical details. Headlines are tight, oversized, and sentence-cased; labels are uppercase with generous tracking; body copy stays readable at a relaxed line-height.

### Brand Essence
A young Nigerian developer who turns thoughtful ideas into clear, usable digital products for people who care about the details. Personality: observant, precise, inventive.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and quietly confident. CTAs are specific and active; microcopy sounds like a person, not a marketing department.
Example lines: “I build the part people remember.” and “See how the idea became an interface.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A compact monogram mark built from an offset O and E: two open geometric forms joined by a saffron diagonal stroke, suggesting both initials and a forward slash in code. It should work as a bold symbol without relying on text.

### Signature Brand Color
Saffron Signal — #F4B942. It is bright enough to guide attention, grounded enough to feel mature, and distinctive against the ink-black blue foundation.
